## Weather information

Forecasts, air quality and alerts come from third-party services and are estimates. They can be late, incomplete or wrong, and Murk only checks for alerts when it refreshes. Do not rely on Murk for decisions that affect safety. In severe weather, follow official local sources and emergency services.

## Murk Pro

Murk Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription with a monthly and an annual plan. Prices are shown in the app before you buy, in your local currency.

- Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
- The subscription renews automatically for the same period and price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
- If a free trial is offered and you start one, the paid period begins when the trial ends unless you cancel before then.
- Manage or cancel in iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or with "Manage Subscription" in Murk. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
- Refunds are handled by Apple: <https://reportaproblem.apple.com>
- "Restore Purchases" in Murk reactivates Murk Pro on any device signed in to the same Apple ID. No separate account is needed.

Everything that isn't marked Pro stays free: current conditions, the 8-day forecast, Current Location and one saved city, widgets, and official weather alerts.
